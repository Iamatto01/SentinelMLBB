“A mysterious astrologer.”

Esmeralda is a Tank/Mage hero primarily played in the Exp Lane lane. In the current meta, Esmeralda holds a 43.0% win rate with a 0.4% pick rate across all ranks. Esmeralda is strong against Lolita, Argus, Marcel, but struggles against Karrie, Lunox, Dyrroth. Best paired with Suyou, Bane, Aulus for maximum team synergy.

Strong Against

Lolita+4.8%Argus+3.5%Marcel+3.3%Mathilda+3.2%Lapu-Lapu+3.2%

Esmeralda works well against heroes with shields, like Akai and Lolita, because her skills not only ignore their shields, but she also converts them into her own.

Weak Against

Karrie+2.8%Lunox+2.7%Dyrroth+2.6%Natan+2.5%Fredrinn+2.5%

Esmeralda is countered by highly mobile Marksmen, such as Karrie and Lesley, because she lacks the burst damage to finish them off quickly and can't keep up with their mobility.

Combos

TEAMFIGHT
→
→
→

In teamfights, enter the fight using Esmeralda's Ultimate, then use her 1st Skill to gain Movement Speed and steal the shields of nearby enemies, before finishing them off with her 2nd Skill and Basic Attacks.

LANING
→
→
→
→
→

In the laning phase, use Esmeralda's 1st Skill to approach the enemy and hit them with her 2nd Skill followed by Basic Attacks. Her 1st Skill should be up again soon thanks to the CD reduction from her 2nd Skill, so use her 1st Skill again for Movement Speed and shields, and continue using her 2nd Skill and Basic Attacks.

BuffShield
Frostmoon Shield

CD: 8.5 Mana Cost: 40

Esmeralda gains 400 (+150% Total Magic Power) Shield and 40% Movement Speed which rapidly decays over its duration for 4s. Meanwhile, she gradually transforms the shields of her nearby enemies into her own shield, capped at 50% of her Max HP.

ShieldBuff
Stardust Dance

CD: 4.5 Mana Cost: 35

Esmeralda waves Stardust and Frostmoon, dealing 240 (+60% Total Physical Attack) Physical Damage and 300 (+120% Total Magic Power) Magic Damage to nearby enemies. Each time she deals damage to a hero, the cooldown of Frostmoon Shield is reduced by 1.5s (damaging non-hero units grants a 0.5s reduction) and the enemy's Movement Speed is reduced by 10% for 1.5s.

AOEBuff
Falling Starmoon

CD: 30 Mana Cost: 100

Esmeralda coheres the power of Astrospace into her two weapons - Stardust and Frostmoon. The longer this skill is channeled, the longer the cast range. Use Again: Esmeralda casts Stardust at the target location, dealing 350 (+90% Total Physical Attack) Physical Damage, and then blinks to the target location and casts Frostmoon, dealing 350 (+140% Total Magic Power) Magic Damage and immobilizing enemies in the area for 1s.
